# Independent review template

Template only. No actual review, execution, finding or acceptance is recorded here.

## Identity and independence

Record phase/wave, exact author base/head and all material authors/families;
reviewer requested/effective route and scope competence; actual Task/Dispatch,
worktree and turn evidence; independence and conflict-of-interest disposition.

## Scope and method

Record reviewed paths/commits, relevant source/callers/contracts, environment,
assertion analysis, actual commands and raw evidence. Separate observed behavior,
inference and unexecuted expectations. Check generated/out-of-scope changes.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Exact source location | Violated invariant and impact | Reproduction/evidence | Author-owned correction | Current disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Retain original reports and rejected claims. Reviewers do not edit author source.

## Actual verification

Record command/environment/exit status, actual test methods or assertions with
accurate units, and material omissions. Where needed, show reached meaningful
negative assertions, equivalent controls and exact restoration. Passing probes
that assert a defect do not demonstrate protection or implementation acceptance.

## Final decision

Not reviewed. Replace with an explicit actual verdict against the exact reviewed
head, findings resolved/unresolved, remaining scope limits and combined-source
review requirements. Do not infer production readiness, broader reviewer
qualification or lifecycle completion from acceptance of a bounded package.
